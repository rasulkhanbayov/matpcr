 A. BLOCKING (resolve before submission; need raw data / reanalysis)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
BL1. UNIT OF ANALYSIS (seed pseudoreplication). The S=3 seeds run on the
     same 30 items, yet the McNemar tests and bootstrap CIs are computed over
     pooled "records" (e.g. 555, 2,026), treating seed replicates of one item
     as independent. This makes every p-value anti-conservative and every CI
     too narrow. Re-run all McNemar p-values and bootstrap CIs at the ITEM
     level (aggregate seeds within item, then cluster/bootstrap by item and,
     where items share a source, by source). The large CGSV effects will
     likely survive; the reported p=3e-8-type values will not stand as-is.
     LOCATION: Results/CGSV, Table 3, Methods protocol (red-flagged).

BL2. VERIFIER IN-DISTRIBUTION SPLIT LEAKAGE. The "random 80/20" verifier
     split is record-level, so seed replicates and same-chain claims of one
     item can land in both train and test, inflating the in-distribution
     AUC (0.858) that anchors contribution (4). Re-split disjoint at the
     item/parent-source level and re-report P/R/F1/AUC/ECE. The held-out
     numbers are unaffected. LOCATION: Results/Verifier (red-flagged).

BL3. DFT-ORACLE VALIDITY INCOMPLETE + CO-AUTHOR SIGN-OFF. The DFT precision
     audit (Exp 1 Half-B) is done only for the 3 geometric families; the 3
     DFT families and the DFT reference protocol are unvalidated. All
     DFT/All-column numbers, the stability accuracy arm, and the
     metallicity/magnetism verifier arms are provisional until M.K. signs
     off. Also confirm the magnetization sign convention (total magnetization
     is conventionally >=0, so the "sign" check may be a magnitude check) and
     that zero_atol matches real DFT noise (~1e-3, not 1e-6).
     LOCATION: Table 1 [pending], Methods DFT oracles (red-flagged).

BL4. CGSV MECHANISM NOT ISOLATED. CGSV is scored by the same oracle that
     supplies its feedback, so a PCR gain (and b=0) is consistent with the
     model RETRACTING/hedging a flagged claim as much as correcting the
     physics. Report the checkable-claim counts c_i before and after CGSV to
     separate correction from retraction. Until then CGSV is an
     oracle-actionability control, not a demonstrated correction capability.
     LOCATION: Results/CGSV (red-flagged).
